# Ideas to look up later (not in the design yet)

## Body heat at night (added 2026-10-27)
- What: a small heat-to-power chip (thermoelectric, "TEG") on skin, with a metal fin to cool air, charges while worn/sleeping.
- Rough numbers (estimate, unverified): 5-20 uW for a small patch; 8 h = 0.04-0.16 mWh = 20-80% of one day of music (~0.2 mWh).
- Earlier "0.3 mW" claim was too high. PLAN.md says ~1 uW.
- Needs: skin contact (sleeve clip or wristband); weak under a thick blanket.
- To check later: real TEG chips (e.g. Micropelt, Laird, Marlow), AEM10941 or LTC3108 can start from low voltage; size/cost; measure on step 3.
- Decision: not added. Revisit after we measure step 3.

## Other night sources (all tiny, low priority)
- Movement while sleeping ~1-3 uW; radio ~0.01-1 uW (our Wi-Fi measure: 0.23 uW); LED night light next to the solar cell (needs a fixed spot = a habit).
